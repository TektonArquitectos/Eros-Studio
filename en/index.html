FAQ

What people ask me before deciding

What exactly does your service include?

It depends on where your business is. It can range from a diagnosis with an action plan, to a bilingual website with a lead-capture system, to monthly strategic support. In the free diagnosis I tell you what you need and what you don't.

How much does it cost?

Each project is quoted by scope. You get an exact price in writing after the diagnosis, so you can decide with clarity and no pressure.

How long does it take?

A diagnosis, one week. A complete digital presence, 2 to 4 weeks. An international lead-capture system, 4 to 6. Ongoing support is monthly.

What other services do you offer?

Beyond strategy and digital presence: drone footage of properties, sites and hotels; professional-grade video editing for social media and web; architecture and interior photography; 360° virtual tours; optimised Google Business profile; social media management; Meta and Google ad campaigns; and bilingual copywriting for your brand. All designed so an international client sees you and trusts you.

Do you work with businesses outside Latin America?

Yes. I work remotely with clients from any country, in Spanish or English.

How do we measure results?

From day one we define what we'll measure: enquiries, WhatsApp conversations, bookings or appointments. Every month you see what's working, in language you understand.

What if I don't like the result?

Revision rounds are included and we work together at every stage. Nothing launches until you're happy.

Do you offer ongoing support afterwards?

Yes. It's optional and it's where my clients grow the most: maintenance, content, optimisation and monthly strategy.
